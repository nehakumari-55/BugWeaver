MY CAREER STORY

Quality is not the final checkpoint.
It is how trust is built.

I began my career close to the evidence: writing test cases, interrogating databases, and tracing whether business rules survived every transaction. That foundation taught me that the most valuable QA professionals do more than find defects. They make risk visible early enough for teams to act.

As the systems grew more complex, so did my role. I moved from test execution into integration assurance, API verification, automation, migration validation, and go-live readiness. Today, I connect technical detail with business consequence—helping developers, business representatives, and service providers reach a shared, evidence-based release decision.
